Income is money recorded as received. Forecasts include configured monthly fees even before invoices exist; they are estimates, not received income. Open a client account to configure its fee or record payments.

OPEN CLIENT ACCOUNT

Account & payments

Unpaid invoices block workspace access after the due date plus seven complete UTC calendar days. Full settlement restores access automatically. Licence expiry and manual inactive status remain separate.

Monthly billing & forecast

Monthly invoices are automatic and follow the approved tier. Existing test clients show simulated TEST / DEMO invoices for presentations only: no emails, real collection or access blocking. New clients use live billing. Manually inactive clients pause invoicing. Service-month invoices are not counted twice; platform totals exclude test accounts.

Debtor profile

Payment terms suggest a due date for new invoices. They do not change existing invoices or the seven-day blocking grace period. Internal notes are not shown to clients. No emails are sent automatically.

Create invoice

Set up seller and client billing details once. All invoices reuse the same standard layout and saved details; only change the amount, description or dates when needed. Submitting records the charge on the client account, regardless of email delivery.

For a monthly service fee, select its month. Leave blank for extra/one-off charges. One active monthly service invoice is allowed per month.

Record received payment

Allocate payments to the relevant invoice. Split a payment across invoices using a distinct reference per allocation.

Record advance payment / prepaid credit

Record money actually received. Credit pays existing unpaid invoices oldest-due first, then remains available for future invoices. It counts as income only once, when received.

Supervisor payment agreement / unblock

Does not erase debt or bypass an expired licence or manually inactive account. Access blocks again when this dated agreement expires unless the overdue invoices are paid.

Correct an invoice or payment

Corrections retain history. Reverse payments before voiding their invoice. Reversing an advance receipt also reverses its invoice allocations and may restore arrears.

OPEN CLIENT ACCOUNT

Client operators & access

Owners and platform staff can add client staff, including Office Administrators, and assign buildings and sections. Office Administrators default to Leasing and Billing; they do not need unit numbers or resident details. Students and tenants are created and managed only in the client app. The main client administrator retains all client buildings and is protected. Platform staff roles are managed separately in PrimeOps team.

Invoice seller & delivery setup

One-time setup: save the actual invoicing entity here. These details and the standard invoice layout are reused automatically for future invoices. Change them only when the business particulars change; already issued invoices keep their original details. This template supports South African full-invoice fields; verify tax status, rates and particulars with your accountant. It is not a guarantee of legal compliance.

Invoice amounts are final totals including VAT where applicable. Client details come from their reviewed billing profile. Configure SMTP and Payfast merchant credentials privately through server environment variables, never in chat. Test payments never settle live debt.
